Questions

Does the trial need a credit card?

No. Just download, install and get your key by email. No card, no payment details.

Is my data stored online?

No. Everything is stored on your own computer. There is no cloud account and no subscription.

Do I need internet to use the software?

No. Only the one-time trial activation needs internet. After that you can work fully offline.

Do I need a barcode scanner?

No. Mizan Suite can scan barcodes with any webcam, and also works with USB scanners and even just typing codes by hand.

What happens after the trial?

Your data is safe and stays on your PC. Buy a license to keep using it, or just uninstall.

Can I migrate from another software?

Yes. You can import product lists and supplier invoices from Excel files.
