Connect your phone

Open the app on your phone

Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi / network as this PC, then:

  1. Scan the QR code with the phone camera, or open the link below.
  2. The first time, the phone shows a security warning. This is normal: tap "Advanced" then "Proceed anyway" (only once).
  3. Log in with your account PIN, the same one used on this PC.
  4. Done - the phone dashboard opens.
